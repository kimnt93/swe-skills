### Investigation

**Read-only. The deliverable is a cited answer.** No PR.

1. Restate the question. If it is vague, state your reading and go.
2. "How does it work" → the **how** skill. "Why is it like this" → the **why** skill. Both when
   the answer feeds a change.
3. Cite every claim with `file:line`, a commit, a PR or a log line. Say what you could not find.
4. If the answer shows a bug or a needed change, stop and re-route to [bug-fix](bug-fix.md) or
   [feature](feature.md) with the evidence.

**Reply:** the answer first, then the evidence, then what is still unknown.
